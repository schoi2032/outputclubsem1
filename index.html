00 Wicky
For CI students and teachers

School chat, finally calm.

Wicky replaces Google Chat with something smaller, safer and alot more thoughtful. Built by students in Output Club, for the people who actually use it every day.

Log in with school Google Scroll to explore
01 / Small by design

Three people. Thats the whole chat.

Every chat caps at three students, so nobody gets lost in a 40 person thread. When a teacher joins the limit lifts, and the teacher is always the owner of the chat.

02 / Moderated quietly

Safe without feeling watched.

Swear words get censored before they land. AI moderation catches what a word list cant, the same message sent three or four times gets stopped, and any message or user can be reported in two taps.

03 / WICKYpedia

Ask CI anything.

Our own AI knows the schedule, the clubs and where room 3204 is hiding. Every AI written message carries a tag, so you always know weather a real person said it.

04 / Made by people

Stickers drawn by hand, not prompts.

Every Wicky sticker and GIF is drawn by someone in our club. No AI slop, ever. Emoji reactions are limited too, so a conversation stays a conversation and not a pile of thumbs.

05 / The small stuff

And the small stuff that makes it work.

A

Teachers come first

Messages from teachers get higher notification priority, so the important ones never get burried.

B

A chat with yourself

Links, reminders, half finished thoughts. Your own private space thats always there.

C

Chats always on the side

Your chats never disappear. Go home to see every new message at once or search to find an old one.

D

Make it yours

Themes, colors and layout are yours to change. Its your chat after all.
